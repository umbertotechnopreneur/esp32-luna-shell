# Meet Cody

**VibeWare · Cody Companion**

**Author:** Umberto Giacobbi · **Created:** 2026-10-05 · **Updated:** 2026-11-12 · **Revision:** 1.0

Cody is a small character that can blink, move, react and bring a little warmth to the workday. Its behavior is artistic, not a diagnosis of a person's mood or wellbeing.

## A little character

In the current source, Cody has idle gestures and can react to eligible touch or to observed Codex activity. Some expressions are deliberately subtle; sounds follow the user's settings and quiet hours. Reactions depend on the current screen, build and actual available information. Real-device appearance, timing and audio still need verification.

## Idle scenes

When appropriate, Cody can choose from small idle gestures such as a yawn, whistle or greeting. Standby and other important experiences take priority. Showcase poses on Home are demonstrations; they do not by themselves mean that Codex is listening or working.

## Personalization

The character has eight named color themes, starting with the original Classic
appearance. The Settings preview shows four representative color swatches;
these themes do not change the interface palette. Idle display, optional event
reactions and parallax are separate choices. The physical appearance, motion
and resource use still require device verification.

## A work in progress

The source includes an experimental, session-only artistic happiness model. It is not a wellbeing service and does not infer human fatigue. The native wellbeing integration is deferred.
