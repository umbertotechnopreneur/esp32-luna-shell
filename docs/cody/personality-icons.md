# Cody's expressions

**VibeWare · Cody Companion**

**Author:** Umberto Giacobbi · **Created:** 2026-10-04 · **Updated:** 2026-11-12 · **Revision:** 1.0

Cody has a visual vocabulary of small symbols and expressions. They can add personality or draw attention to a real device event, while the screen remains clear and usable.

## Reading the room

A symbol may reflect an eligible character moment or a current device observation, such as a connection or battery state. Availability depends on the screen and source data. A missing or stale observation must not be dressed up as a current fact.

Sounds, when available, follow Sounds and quiet hours. Setup, important questions and power transitions have their own priorities. Visual design and behavior still need real-device verification.
